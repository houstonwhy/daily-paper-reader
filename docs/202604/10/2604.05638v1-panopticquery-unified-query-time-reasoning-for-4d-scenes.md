## 摘要
通过自然语言查询理解动态 4D 环境，不仅需要准确的场景重建，还需要在空间、时间和视角上实现鲁棒的语义对齐（semantic grounding）。尽管近期使用神经表示的方法推动了 4D 重建的发展，但它们在上下文推理方面仍存在局限，特别是在处理交互、时序动作和空间关系等复杂语义时。一个关键挑战在于如何将噪声较多且依赖视角的预测转化为全局一致的 4D 解释。我们提出了 PanopticQuery，这是一个用于 4D 场景统一查询时推理的框架。我们的方法基于 4D 高斯泼溅（4D Gaussian Splatting）进行高保真动态重建，并引入了一种多视角语义共识机制，通过聚合多个视角和时间帧的 2D 语义预测，实现自然语言查询的对齐。该过程过滤了不一致的输出，增强了几何一致性，并通过神经场优化将 2D 语义提升为结构化的 4D 对齐结果。为了支持评估，我们提出了 Panoptic-L4D，这是一个用于动态场景中基于语言查询的新基准。实验表明，PanopticQuery 在复杂语言查询方面达到了新的最先进水平，能够有效处理属性、动作、空间关系和多物体交互。补充材料中提供了视频演示。

## Abstract
Understanding dynamic 4D environments through natural language queries requires not only accurate scene reconstruction but also robust semantic grounding across space, time, and viewpoints. While recent methods using neural representations have advanced 4D reconstruction, they remain limited in contextual reasoning, especially for complex semantics such as interactions, temporal actions, and spatial relations. A key challenge lies in transforming noisy, view-dependent predictions into globally consistent 4D interpretations. We introduce PanopticQuery, a framework for unified query-time reasoning in 4D scenes. Our approach builds on 4D Gaussian Splatting for high-fidelity dynamic reconstruction and introduces a multi-view semantic consensus mechanism that grounds natural language queries by aggregating 2D semantic predictions across multiple views and time frames. This process filters inconsistent outputs, enforces geometric consistency, and lifts 2D semantics into structured 4D groundings via neural field optimization. To support evaluation, we present Panoptic-L4D, a new benchmark for language-based querying in dynamic scenes. Experiments demonstrate that PanopticQuery sets a new state of the art on complex language queries, effectively handling attributes, actions, spatial relationships, and multi-object interactions.
